An EMI (equated monthly instalment) is a fixed monthly payment that covers both interest and principal. Early EMIs are mostly interest; later ones mostly principal.

## What drives the EMI
Loan amount, interest rate and tenure. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises total interest paid.

## Example
₹10 lakh at 9% a year: about ₹20,700 a month for 5 years (total interest about ₹2.4 lakh) or about ₹12,700 for 10 years (total interest about ₹5.2 lakh).

## Good practice
- Many lenders consider total EMIs above about 40-50% of take-home pay as risky.
- Prepaying costly loans early saves the most interest.
- Credit card debt and personal loans are usually the most expensive; clearing them often "returns" more than investing.
- Floating-rate home loans are linked to an external benchmark such as the RBI repo rate, so EMIs or tenure change when rates change.
