A credit score is a three-digit number from 300 to 900 that summarises how reliably you repay loans and credit cards. Credit bureaus in India include TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark. Lenders use it to decide whether to lend and at what interest rate. Scores of about 750 and above are generally considered good.

## What affects it
- Payment history: late or missed EMIs and card dues hurt the most.
- Credit utilisation: using a large share of your card limit (often above 30%) lowers the score.
- Credit mix and age: a long history with both secured and unsecured credit helps.
- Many recent loan applications (hard enquiries) can lower it.

## Tips
Pay on time with auto-debit, keep utilisation low, do not close your oldest card without reason, and check your report once a year for errors. Checking your own score does not reduce it.
