---
title: Credit cards: useful but costly if misused
category: banking
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: RBI financial education)
reference_url: https://www.rbi.org.in/financialeducation/
---
A credit card gives an interest-free period (often up to about 45-50 days) if you pay the full bill by the due date.

- Paying only the "minimum due" leaves the rest charged at very high interest, often around 36-45% a year, plus GST on charges.
- Cash withdrawals attract interest from day one.
- Used well, cards build credit history and give rewards; used badly, they create expensive debt.
Rule of thumb: never spend on a card what you could not pay from your bank account this month.
