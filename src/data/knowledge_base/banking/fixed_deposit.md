A fixed deposit locks money with a bank or NBFC for a chosen period (7 days to 10 years) at a fixed interest rate.

## Key points
- Rates are fixed at the start; senior citizens usually get about 0.25-0.50% extra.
- Premature withdrawal is usually allowed with a small penalty.
- Interest is taxable at your slab rate every year, and TDS applies when interest crosses a threshold. Submitting Form 15G/15H avoids TDS if your total income is below the taxable limit.
- Bank FDs are covered by DICGC insurance up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank. Corporate FDs are not insured; check their credit rating.
- A 5-year tax-saver FD qualifies for the old-regime 80C deduction but has a 5-year lock-in.

FDs give certainty but, after tax and inflation, real returns can be close to zero.
