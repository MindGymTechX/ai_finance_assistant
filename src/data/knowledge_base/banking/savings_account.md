A savings account is a bank deposit for day-to-day money, with easy withdrawals through UPI, cards and net banking.

## Key points
- Interest is usually low (often 2.5-4% a year at large banks) and is taxable at your slab rate; a deduction for savings interest exists under the old tax regime.
- Deposits are insured by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank (principal plus interest).
- Watch for minimum balance requirements and charges.
- A sweep-in facility moves surplus above a limit into a fixed deposit to earn more while staying accessible.

Savings accounts suit emergency funds and spending money, not long-term growth.
