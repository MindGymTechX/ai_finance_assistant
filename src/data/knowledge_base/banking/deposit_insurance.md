The Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), an RBI subsidiary, insures bank deposits up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, covering principal and interest together. A proposal to raise the limit (reported as ₹7.5 lakh) was under consideration in 2026 but had not been notified when this article was checked. It covers savings, current, fixed and recurring deposits in commercial banks, small finance banks, regional rural banks and most cooperative banks.

- The limit is per bank, not per branch: two accounts at different branches of the same bank share one ₹5 lakh limit.
- Deposits in different banks are insured separately.
- NBFC deposits, corporate FDs and mutual funds are not covered.
