title: Recurring deposits (RD)
category: banking
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: RBI financial education)
reference_url: https://www.rbi.org.in/financialeducation/
---
A recurring deposit lets you deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed term (6 months to 10 years), earning interest similar to an FD.

- Good for building a habit and for short-term goals with a known date (a trip, a gadget, a festival).
- Interest is taxable at slab rates; TDS rules are similar to FDs.
- Missing instalments can attract a penalty.
Compared with an SIP in an equity fund, an RD gives certainty but lower expected growth; compared with an SIP in a debt fund, taxation and liquidity differ.
