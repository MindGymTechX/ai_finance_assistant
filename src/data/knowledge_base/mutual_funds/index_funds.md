An index fund copies an index such as the Nifty 50 instead of picking stocks. It buys the same companies in the same weights.

## Why many beginners use them
- Very low expense ratios.
- Instant diversification across the index's companies.
- No risk of a fund manager under-performing the market by bad picks; you get the market's return minus costs.

## Things to check
- Tracking error: how closely it follows the index.
- Which index: Nifty 50 is large-cap; Nifty Next 50, midcap and smallcap indices are more volatile.
An index fund still falls when the market falls.
