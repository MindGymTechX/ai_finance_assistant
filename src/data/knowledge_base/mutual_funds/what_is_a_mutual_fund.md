A mutual fund pools money from many investors and a professional fund manager invests it in stocks, bonds or other assets according to a stated objective. Each investor owns units; the value of one unit is the NAV.

## Why people use them
- Diversification with small amounts (SIPs from a few hundred rupees).
- Professional management and SEBI regulation.
- Liquidity: most open-ended funds can be redeemed on any business day.

## Costs and risks
Funds charge an annual expense ratio. Returns are not guaranteed; equity funds can fall sharply. Every scheme shows a riskometer from Low to Very High.

"Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related documents carefully" is the mandated reminder that past performance does not guarantee future returns.
