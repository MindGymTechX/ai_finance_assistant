NAV is the per-unit value of a mutual fund: (market value of all holdings + cash − liabilities) ÷ number of units. It is published every business day.

## Common myths
- A fund with NAV ₹10 is not "cheaper" than one with NAV ₹500. What matters is the percentage change in NAV, i.e. returns.
- A new fund offer (NFO) at ₹10 is not a bargain for that reason.

## Cut-off times
Purchases are allotted at the NAV of the day the money reaches the fund before the cut-off time (3 pm for most schemes), otherwise the next day's NAV.
