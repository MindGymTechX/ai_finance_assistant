SEBI groups schemes into categories so investors can compare like with like.

## Equity funds (mostly stocks)
Large cap, mid cap, small cap, flexi cap, multi cap, large & mid cap, focused, value/contra, sectoral/thematic and ELSS (tax-saving with a 3-year lock-in).

## Debt funds (bonds and money-market instruments)
Liquid and overnight funds (very short-term, used for parking cash), money market, short duration, corporate bond, gilt (government bonds) and others. Risk comes from interest-rate changes and the credit quality of borrowers.

## Hybrid funds
Mix equity and debt: conservative hybrid, balanced advantage (changes the mix dynamically), aggressive hybrid, equity savings and arbitrage funds.

## Others
Index funds, ETFs, fund of funds, and solution-oriented funds for retirement or children's goals.
