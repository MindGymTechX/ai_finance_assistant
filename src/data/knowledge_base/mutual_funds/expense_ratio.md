The Total Expense Ratio (TER) is the yearly fee a mutual fund charges, as a percentage of your investment. It is deducted daily from the NAV, so you never see a separate bill.

## Typical ranges
Index funds and ETFs often charge about 0.05-0.5%; actively managed equity funds about 0.5-2.25%. SEBI caps TER by fund size and type.

## Why it matters
On ₹10 lakh over 20 years at 12% gross return, a 0.5% TER leaves about ₹88 lakh, while a 1.5% TER leaves about ₹74 lakh. Costs compound like returns.

Lower cost alone does not make a fund suitable, but between similar funds, cost is one of the few things known in advance.
