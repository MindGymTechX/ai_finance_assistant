Under the old tax regime, investments and payments up to ₹1.5 lakh a year reduce taxable income. This was Section 80C of the 1961 Act; the Income-tax Act, 2025 carries a similar deduction under a new section number. It is not available in the new regime.

## Common eligible options
- EPF (employee contribution) and voluntary PF.
- PPF: 15-year government scheme, tax-free interest.
- ELSS mutual funds: equity, shortest lock-in (3 years).
- 5-year tax-saver bank FD.
- National Savings Certificate (NSC).
- Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (for a girl child).
- Life insurance premiums, home-loan principal repayment, children's tuition fees.

## Choosing
Match the product to the goal: ELSS has equity risk and growth potential; PPF and SSY are safe with long lock-ins; FDs and NSC are fixed-return with taxable or partly taxable interest. Buying insurance only to save tax often leads to poor-value policies.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
