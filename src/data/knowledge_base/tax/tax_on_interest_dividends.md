title: Tax on interest and dividends
category: tax
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: Income Tax Department, India)

---
- Bank FD and RD interest: added to your income and taxed at your slab rate every year, even if you receive it only at maturity. Banks deduct TDS when interest crosses a threshold; Form 15G/15H can prevent TDS if your total income is below the taxable limit.
- Savings account interest: taxable at slab rate; a limited deduction exists under the old regime.
- Dividends from shares and mutual fund IDCW payouts: taxable at slab rate; TDS applies above a yearly threshold per payer.
- PPF interest is tax-free; EPF interest is tax-free within limits.
High-tax-bracket investors therefore often compare post-tax returns, not headline rates.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
