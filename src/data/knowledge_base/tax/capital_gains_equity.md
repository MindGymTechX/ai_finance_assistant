When you sell listed shares or equity-oriented mutual funds (65%+ in Indian equity) at a profit, the gain is taxed. Securities Transaction Tax (STT) must have been paid.

## Short-term capital gains (STCG)
Held for 12 months or less: taxed at 20%.

## Long-term capital gains (LTCG)
Held for more than 12 months: gains up to ₹1.25 lakh in a year are exempt; gains above that are taxed at 12.5% (plus surcharge and cess), without indexation. These rates applied from 23 July 2024 and Budget 2026 left them unchanged.

## Example
You sell equity fund units held for 3 years with a ₹2,00,000 gain. ₹1,25,000 is exempt; tax is 12.5% of ₹75,000 = ₹9,375 (before cess).

## Notes
- Losses can be set off against gains: short-term losses against both STCG and LTCG; long-term losses only against LTCG, and carried forward up to 8 years if the return is filed on time.
- Shares bought before 1 Feb 2018 have "grandfathering" of gains up to that date.
- From Budget 2026, proceeds from share buybacks are treated as capital gains for shareholders.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
