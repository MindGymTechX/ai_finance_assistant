PPF is a government-backed long-term savings scheme available at post offices and banks.

## Key features
- Tenure: 15 years, extendable in blocks of 5 years.
- Deposit: minimum ₹500 and maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year.
- Interest: set by the government every quarter, calculated monthly and compounded yearly. It has been 7.1% a year for many consecutive quarters, including October-December 2026.
- Tax: "EEE" — deposits qualify for the old-regime tax-saving deduction, interest is tax-free and the maturity amount is tax-free.
- Liquidity: partial withdrawal allowed after 5 complete financial years (that is, from the 7th financial year), up to a limit; loans available from the 3rd to the 6th year.
- Safety: backed by the Government of India.

## Who it suits
Conservative investors and the debt part of a long-term portfolio. The long lock-in makes it unsuitable for money needed within a few years.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
