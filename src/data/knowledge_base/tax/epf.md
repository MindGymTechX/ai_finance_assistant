title: Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)

EPF is a mandatory retirement savings scheme for many salaried employees, run by EPFO.
- Contributions: typically 12% of basic pay + dearness allowance from the employee, matched by the employer (part of the employer share goes to the Employees' Pension Scheme).
- Interest: declared every year by EPFO; it has been around 8% in recent years.
- Tax: employee contributions qualify for the old-regime tax-saving deduction. Interest on employee contributions above ₹2.5 lakh a year is taxable. Withdrawals after 5 years of continuous service are tax-free.
- Withdrawals: allowed for specific needs (house, medical, education, marriage) and on leaving employment, subject to rules.
- Voluntary PF (VPF) lets employees contribute more at the same interest rate.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
