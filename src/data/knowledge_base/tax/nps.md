NPS is a government-regulated, market-linked retirement scheme managed by PFRDA-registered pension fund managers.

## How it works
- Tier I is the retirement account with restricted withdrawals; Tier II is an optional, flexible account.
- You choose the asset mix (equity, corporate bonds, government bonds) yourself (active choice) or use life-cycle funds that reduce equity with age (auto choice).
- Costs are low compared with most investment products.

## Tax benefits
- Under the old regime: contributions count within the ₹1.5 lakh tax-saving limit, plus an additional ₹50,000 deduction exclusive to NPS.
- Employer contributions to NPS get a deduction under both regimes, up to a percentage of salary.

## At retirement
Part of the corpus can be withdrawn as a lump sum and the rest must buy an annuity (pension). PFRDA changed the exit rules in December 2025: non-government subscribers (All Citizen and corporate models) can now take up to 80% as a lump sum, with at least 20% used for an annuity (earlier the split was 60:40). Government employees remain on 60:40. Very small corpora can be withdrawn fully, and the exit age can be deferred up to 85. Conditions apply, so check the current PFRDA regulations. Annuity income is taxable as income.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
