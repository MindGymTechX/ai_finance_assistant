## Debt mutual funds
Units of debt funds (and other "specified" funds with 35% or less in Indian equity) bought on or after 1 April 2023 are taxed at your income slab rate on gains, whatever the holding period. There is no long-term benefit or indexation for these.

## Gold and other listed assets
Listed securities such as gold ETFs are long-term after 12 months; unlisted assets such as physical gold and gold funds of funds generally after 24 months. Long-term gains on these are taxed at 12.5% without indexation; short-term gains at slab rates.

## Property
Long-term after 24 months, taxed at 12.5% without indexation, with a transition option for individuals on property bought before 23 July 2024. Reinvestment exemptions exist for buying another house or specified bonds, subject to conditions.

Holding-period and rate rules changed in 2023-2024, so the purchase date matters.
