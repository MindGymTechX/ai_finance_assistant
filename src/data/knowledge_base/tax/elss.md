Equity Linked Savings Schemes (ELSS) are diversified equity mutual funds whose investments qualify for the old-regime tax-saving deduction (up to ₹1.5 lakh together with other eligible items).

- Lock-in: 3 years, the shortest among tax-saving options. For SIPs, each instalment is locked for 3 years from its own date.
- Returns: market-linked, not guaranteed; can be negative over short periods.
- Tax on exit: gains are taxed as equity LTCG (12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh per year), since units are always held over 12 months.
- Under the new regime there is no deduction, but ELSS remains an ordinary equity fund.

ELSS vs PPF: ELSS offers growth potential with equity risk and a 3-year lock-in; PPF offers safety and tax-free returns with a 15-year term.

Rules as of: Finance Act 2026 (tax year 2026-27, Income-tax Act, 2025). Tax rules change; confirm on incometax.gov.in or with a tax professional before filing.
