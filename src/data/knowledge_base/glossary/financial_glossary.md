Plain-language definitions of common finance terms.

## AMC
AMC: Asset Management Company: the company that runs mutual fund schemes.

## Annuity
Annuity: A product, usually bought from an insurer, that converts a lump sum into regular income, often for life.

## Asset allocation
Asset allocation: How money is divided among equity, debt, gold and cash.

## Bear market
Bear market: A prolonged market decline, commonly defined as a fall of 20% or more from a peak.

## Benchmark
Benchmark: An index used to judge a fund's performance, such as the Nifty 50 for a large-cap fund.

## Beta
Beta: How much a stock or fund tends to move relative to the market; above 1 means more volatile than the market.

## Blue chip
Blue chip: A large, well-established company with a long record of stable earnings.

## Bonus shares
Bonus shares: Extra shares given free to existing shareholders in a fixed ratio; the price adjusts so total value is unchanged.

## Bull market
Bull market: A prolonged period of rising prices.

## CAGR
CAGR: Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly rate that would take an investment from its start value to its end value.

## Capital gain
Capital gain: Profit from selling an asset for more than its purchase price.

## CDSL / NSDL
CDSL / NSDL: India's two depositories, which hold shares and other securities electronically in demat form.

## Corpus
Corpus: The total amount accumulated for a goal, such as a retirement corpus.

## Coupon
Coupon: The fixed interest rate a bond pays on its face value.

## Credit rating
Credit rating: An assessment (AAA, AA, A and so on) of a borrower's ability to repay; lower ratings mean higher default risk.

## Demat account
Demat account: An account that holds shares and securities electronically.

## Derivatives (F&O)
Derivatives (F&O): Futures and options: contracts whose value depends on an underlying asset; leveraged and high-risk for individuals.

## Diversification
Diversification: Spreading money across investments so one loss does not hurt the whole portfolio.

## Drawdown
Drawdown: The fall from a peak to a trough in an investment's value.

## EMI
EMI: Equated monthly instalment: a fixed monthly loan repayment covering interest and principal.

## EPS
EPS: Earnings per share: a company's net profit divided by its number of shares.

## Equity
Equity: Ownership in a company, held through shares.

## Expense ratio
Expense ratio: The yearly fee charged by a mutual fund as a percentage of assets, deducted from NAV.

## Exit load
Exit load: A fee for redeeming mutual fund units before a set period.

## Face value
Face value: The nominal value of a share or bond as stated by the issuer; unrelated to market price for shares.

## Fixed deposit
Fixed deposit: A bank deposit for a fixed term at a fixed interest rate.

## Free float
Free float: Shares available for public trading, excluding promoter and locked-in holdings.

## Fund of funds
Fund of funds: A mutual fund that invests in other mutual funds or ETFs.

## G-sec
G-sec: Government security: a bond issued by the central or a state government.

## Hedge
Hedge: An investment made to reduce the risk of losses in another.

## IDCW
IDCW: Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal: the mutual fund option that pays out distributions (formerly called dividend option).

## Index fund
Index fund: A mutual fund that copies a market index at low cost.

## Inflation
Inflation: The general rise in prices that reduces the buying power of money.

## IPO
IPO: Initial public offering: when a company first sells shares to the public.

## KYC
KYC: Know Your Customer: identity and address verification required to invest.

## Large cap
Large cap: The 100 largest listed companies by market capitalisation (SEBI definition).

## Liquidity
Liquidity: How quickly an asset can be sold at a fair price.

## Lock-in period
Lock-in period: A period during which an investment cannot be withdrawn, such as 3 years for ELSS.

## LTCG
LTCG: Long-term capital gains: gains on assets held longer than the specified period.

## Market capitalisation
Market capitalisation: Share price multiplied by the number of shares outstanding.

## Mid cap
Mid cap: Companies ranked 101 to 250 by market capitalisation (SEBI definition).

## NAV
NAV: Net asset value: the per-unit value of a mutual fund.

## NFO
NFO: New fund offer: the launch period of a new mutual fund scheme.

## Nifty 50
Nifty 50: NSE's index of 50 large, liquid Indian companies.

## P/E ratio
P/E ratio: Share price divided by earnings per share.

## Portfolio
Portfolio: The full set of investments a person holds.

## Price-to-book (P/B)
Price-to-book (P/B): Share price divided by book value per share.

## Rebalancing
Rebalancing: Bringing a portfolio's asset mix back to its target by trimming what grew and adding to what lagged.

## Repo rate
Repo rate: The rate at which the RBI lends to banks; changes ripple through loan and deposit rates.

## Return on equity (ROE)
Return on equity (ROE): Net profit as a percentage of shareholders' equity; a measure of profitability.

## Riskometer
Riskometer: SEBI-mandated scale showing a mutual fund's risk from Low to Very High.

## ROI
ROI: Return on investment: gain relative to the amount invested.

## Rolling returns
Rolling returns: Returns measured over many overlapping periods, showing consistency better than a single period.

## Sensex
Sensex: BSE's index of 30 large Indian companies.

## SIP
SIP: Systematic investment plan: investing a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals.

## Small cap
Small cap: Companies ranked 251 and below by market capitalisation (SEBI definition).

## Standard deviation
Standard deviation: A measure of how widely returns vary from their average; higher means more volatile.

## STCG
STCG: Short-term capital gains: gains on assets held for less than the specified period.

## Stock split
Stock split: Dividing each share into more shares of lower face value; total value is unchanged.

## STP / SWP
STP / SWP: Systematic transfer plan moves money between funds regularly; systematic withdrawal plan pays out a fixed amount regularly.

## STT
STT: Securities transaction tax: a tax on buying and selling listed securities on exchanges.

## TDS
TDS: Tax deducted at source by the payer, such as a bank on FD interest above a threshold.

## Tracking error
Tracking error: How much an index fund's returns differ from its index.

## Volatility
Volatility: How much and how quickly prices move up and down.

## XIRR
XIRR: Extended internal rate of return: the yearly return for investments made at different dates, such as SIPs.

## Yield to maturity (YTM)
Yield to maturity (YTM): The total annual return from a bond if held to maturity at its current price.
