Retirement may last 25-30 years, with no salary and rising medical costs, so it needs the largest corpus of any goal.

## Steps
1. Estimate yearly expenses at retirement in today's money, then inflate them to the retirement date.
2. Estimate the corpus needed: a rough rule is 25-30 times the first year's retirement expenses, depending on returns and inflation assumed after retirement.
3. Count existing EPF, PPF, NPS and other savings.
4. Calculate the monthly investment needed and increase it with salary.

## Common mistakes
Starting late, using retirement savings for other goals, underestimating inflation and health costs, and keeping too little in growth assets early on. EPF alone is often not enough for salaried people.
