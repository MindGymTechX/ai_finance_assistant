SSY is a government savings scheme for a girl child below 10 years of age, opened by a parent or guardian.
- Deposits: minimum ₹250 and maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year, for 15 years from opening.
- Maturity: 21 years from opening; partial withdrawal for education allowed after the girl turns 18.
- Interest: set quarterly by the government, usually a little higher than PPF (8.2% a year for several consecutive quarters through 2026).
- Tax: deposits qualify for the old-regime tax-saving deduction; interest and maturity are tax-free.
It suits the safe portion of a daughter's education or marriage goal.
