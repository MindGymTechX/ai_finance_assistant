An emergency fund is money kept aside for unexpected events: job loss, medical bills, urgent repairs. It is the foundation before investing in volatile assets like stocks.

## How much
A common guideline is 3 to 6 months of essential expenses (rent, EMIs, food, insurance premiums, school fees). People with irregular income or dependants often keep 6 to 12 months.

## Where to keep it
The priority is safety and quick access, not high returns: a savings account, a sweep-in fixed deposit, or a liquid mutual fund. Avoid equity for emergency money because prices may be down exactly when you need cash.

## Why it matters for investing
Without an emergency fund, people are forced to sell investments at a bad time or take expensive loans. Having one lets long-term investments stay invested.
