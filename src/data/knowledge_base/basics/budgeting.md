A budget is a plan for where your income goes. Tracking spending for two or three months usually shows where money leaks.

## The 50/30/20 guideline
- 50% for needs: rent, groceries, utilities, EMIs, insurance.
- 30% for wants: eating out, travel, shopping.
- 20% for savings and investments, including the emergency fund and goals.
It is a starting point, not a rule. In high-rent cities needs may exceed 50%; the habit of saving a fixed share first matters more than the exact split.

## Pay yourself first
Automating savings on salary day (for example an SIP or recurring deposit) means saving happens before spending. Increasing the saved amount whenever income rises (a "step-up") builds wealth without feeling the pinch.
