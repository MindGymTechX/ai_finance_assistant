A financial goal is specific, has an amount and a date: "₹25 lakh for my daughter's college in 8 years", not "save for education".

## Steps
1. List goals and when each is needed (short term under 3 years, medium 3-7, long 7+).
2. Estimate today's cost, then adjust for inflation to the goal date.
3. Count savings already set aside for the goal.
4. Pick an asset mix suited to the horizon and risk appetite.
5. Calculate the monthly SIP needed, and review yearly.

## Priorities
Typical order: emergency fund, adequate health and term insurance, high-interest debt repayment, then goals such as retirement and children's education. Retirement is often under-funded because it feels far away, yet it has no loan option.
