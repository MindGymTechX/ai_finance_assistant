In investing, higher potential returns come with higher risk: a wider range of possible outcomes, including losses.

## The spectrum (roughly, from lower to higher risk)
Savings account and fixed deposits → government bonds and PPF → debt mutual funds → hybrid funds → large-cap equity → mid and small-cap equity → individual stocks → derivatives and crypto.

## Types of risk
- Market risk: prices fall with the overall market.
- Credit risk: a borrower (company or bank) fails to pay.
- Interest-rate risk: bond prices fall when rates rise.
- Inflation risk: returns fail to beat inflation.
- Liquidity risk: you cannot sell quickly at a fair price.
- Concentration risk: too much in one company or sector.

## Time horizon
Equity can fall 20-40% in a bad year, but over long periods (7-10+ years) it has historically been more likely to beat inflation. Money needed within 1-3 years is usually kept in lower-risk options.
