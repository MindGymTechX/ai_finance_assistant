A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals, usually monthly.

## Rupee cost averaging
Because the amount is fixed, you buy more units when prices are low and fewer when prices are high. Over time your average cost per unit tends to be lower than the average price, and you avoid the stress of timing the market.

## Benefits
- Discipline: investing happens automatically.
- Small start: many funds allow SIPs from ₹100-₹500 a month.
- Step-up SIPs increase the amount every year.

## Limits
SIPs do not guarantee profit or protect against loss in a falling market. They work best for long horizons and when continued through downturns. Stopping an SIP during a fall locks in the lower prices without the recovery.
