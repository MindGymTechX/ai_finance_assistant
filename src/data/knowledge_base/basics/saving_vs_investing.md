---
title: Saving, investing and speculating
category: basics
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: SEBI investor education)
reference_url: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/
---
- Saving keeps money safe and available: savings accounts, FDs. Low risk, low return, often below inflation after tax.
- Investing puts money into assets expected to grow over time: equity and debt funds, stocks, bonds. Returns vary, and losses are possible in the short term.
- Speculating bets on short-term price moves: intraday trading, futures and options. SEBI studies have found that a large majority of individual traders in equity F&O lose money.

Beginners usually save for the short term and emergencies, and invest for long-term goals. Speculation is not a plan for goals.
