Inflation is the general rise in prices over time. If inflation is 6% a year, something that costs ₹100 today costs about ₹106 next year. The same rupee buys less in future.

## Real return
The real return is roughly your return minus inflation. A savings account paying 3% while inflation is 6% has a real return of about -3%: your money grows in rupees but loses buying power.

## Planning for goals
Long-term goals must be inflation-adjusted. A college fee of ₹25 lakh today, with 6% yearly inflation, becomes about ₹40 lakh in 8 years and about ₹45 lakh in 10 years. Planning for today's number leaves a large gap.

## In India
The RBI targets consumer price inflation of 4% within a band of 2% to 6%. Education and healthcare costs have often risen faster than general inflation, so goal plans commonly use a higher rate for them.
