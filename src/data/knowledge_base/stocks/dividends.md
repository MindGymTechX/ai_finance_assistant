A dividend is a part of a company's profit paid to shareholders, declared per share (for example ₹10 per share).

- Record date: you must hold the share on this date to receive the dividend.
- Dividend yield = yearly dividend per share ÷ share price.
- In India dividends are taxed in the investor's hands at slab rates, and companies deduct TDS above a yearly threshold.
- A high dividend does not mean a better investment: the share price usually drops by about the dividend amount on the ex-date.
- Mutual funds offer IDCW (income distribution) options; distributions are taxable and reduce NAV, so the growth option is often more tax-efficient for long-term investors.
