An index tracks a group of stocks to show how a market or segment is doing.
- Nifty 50: 50 large, liquid companies listed on NSE.
- Sensex: 30 large companies listed on BSE.
- Sector indices such as Nifty Bank and Nifty IT track one industry.

Both Nifty 50 and Sensex are weighted by free-float market cap, so the biggest companies move the index most.

## Why indices matter
They are benchmarks: a large-cap fund is judged against an index like the Nifty 50. Index funds and ETFs simply copy an index at low cost.
