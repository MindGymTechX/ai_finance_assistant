Market capitalisation (market cap) = share price × number of shares. SEBI classifies listed companies by full market cap rank:
- Large cap: the top 100 companies.
- Mid cap: companies ranked 101 to 250.
- Small cap: companies ranked 251 and below.
AMFI publishes the list twice a year, and mutual fund categories follow it.

## Typical behaviour
Large caps are generally more stable and better researched. Mid and small caps can grow faster but swing much more and can be harder to sell in falling markets. Small-cap indices have historically fallen far more than large-cap indices in market corrections.
