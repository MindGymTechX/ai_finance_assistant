The P/E ratio = share price ÷ earnings per share (EPS). It shows how many rupees investors pay for each rupee of yearly profit.

## Reading it
- A P/E of 25 means investors pay ₹25 for ₹1 of annual earnings.
- A high P/E can mean high expected growth, or an expensive stock. A low P/E can mean a bargain, or a business in trouble.
- Compare P/E within the same industry and against the company's own history, not across unrelated sectors.

## Limits
P/E ignores debt, cash flow quality and one-off profits, and is meaningless for loss-making companies. It is one input in evaluating a stock, never a decision on its own.
