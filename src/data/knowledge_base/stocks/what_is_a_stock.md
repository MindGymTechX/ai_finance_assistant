A share (stock) is a small unit of ownership in a company. If a company has 1 crore shares and you own 1,000, you own 0.01% of it.

## How shareholders earn
- Capital gains: the share price rises above what you paid.
- Dividends: a share of profits the company chooses to pay out.

## Risks
Share prices move with company results, the economy, interest rates and investor mood. A single company can fall sharply or even fail, so holding one or two stocks is much riskier than a diversified fund.

## Equity vs debt
Shareholders are owners: they share in growth but are paid last if the company fails. Bondholders are lenders: they get fixed interest and rank ahead of shareholders.
