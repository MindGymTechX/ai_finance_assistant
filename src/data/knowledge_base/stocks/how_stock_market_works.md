India's main stock exchanges are the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and BSE. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates them.

## What you need
- A demat account (holds shares electronically) with a depository participant; India's depositories are NSDL and CDSL.
- A trading account with a SEBI-registered stockbroker.
- PAN and KYC.

## Trading basics
- Normal trading hours: 9:15 am to 3:30 pm IST, Monday to Friday, except exchange holidays.
- Settlement is T+1: shares and money move one working day after the trade.
- Prices are set by buy and sell orders; a market order executes at the best available price, a limit order only at your price or better.

## Investor protection
Exchanges run investor protection funds, and SEBI's SCORES portal handles complaints against brokers and listed companies.
