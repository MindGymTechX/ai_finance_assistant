Indians traditionally hold gold as jewellery, but there are other ways:
- Physical gold (coins, bars, jewellery): making charges, storage and purity concerns.
- Gold ETFs: track the domestic gold price, held in demat, low cost.
- Gold mutual funds (fund of funds): invest in gold ETFs, allow SIPs without demat.
- Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs): government bonds linked to gold with 2.5% yearly interest; existing bonds trade on exchanges, and new issues have been paused in recent years.

## Role in a portfolio
Gold has often done well when equities or the rupee were weak, so some investors keep a small share (often 5-15%) as a diversifier. It pays no earnings, and its price can stay flat for years. Tax treatment depends on the form and holding period.
