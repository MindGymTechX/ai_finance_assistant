An ETF is a fund that trades on the stock exchange like a share. Most Indian ETFs track an index (Nifty 50, Nifty Bank) or a commodity like gold.

## Compared with index mutual funds
- Bought and sold during market hours at market prices through a demat account.
- Often slightly lower expense ratios, but you pay brokerage and the bid-ask spread.
- Liquidity varies: a thinly traded ETF can trade away from its fair value (iNAV).
Index funds suit SIPs without a demat account; ETFs suit investors who already trade on exchanges.
