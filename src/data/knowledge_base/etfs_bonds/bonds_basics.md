A bond is a loan you give to a government or company. In return you get periodic interest (the coupon) and your principal back at maturity.

## Key terms
- Coupon: the fixed interest rate on face value.
- Yield to maturity (YTM): the total return if held to maturity at today's price.
- Credit rating: AAA is the highest safety; lower ratings pay more because of higher default risk.
- Government securities (G-secs) and treasury bills carry no credit risk. Individuals can buy them directly through RBI Retail Direct.

## Interest rates and prices
When interest rates rise, existing bond prices fall (their fixed coupons look less attractive), and vice versa. Longer-maturity bonds move more. This is why debt funds can show short-term losses.
