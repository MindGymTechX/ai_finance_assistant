Red flags:
- Guaranteed or very high returns ("double money in a year").
- Pressure to act fast, or "insider tips" on WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
- Unregistered advisers: anyone giving paid investment advice in India must be a SEBI-registered investment adviser (RIA) or research analyst. Check the SEBI website.
- Requests to share your demat login, OTP or to transfer money to a personal account.
- "Pump and dump" groups promoting small, illiquid stocks.
Report fraud to SEBI SCORES, the exchanges, or the national cybercrime portal (cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930).
