Volatility is how much prices swing. Equity markets regularly fall: drops of 10% happen often, 20-30% every few years, and larger falls occasionally (for example in 2008 and briefly in 2020).

## Staying invested
Investors who sold during falls often missed the recoveries that followed. Long-term investing works only if you can hold through declines, which is why matching equity exposure to your horizon and tolerance matters.

## Measures
Standard deviation (how widely returns vary), maximum drawdown (largest peak-to-trough fall) and beta (sensitivity to the market) are common risk measures for funds and stocks.
