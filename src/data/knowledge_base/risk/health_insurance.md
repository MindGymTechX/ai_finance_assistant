A medical emergency can wipe out years of savings, so health insurance usually comes before investing.
- Sum insured: many guides suggest at least ₹5-10 lakh per family in cities, with a super top-up for larger cover at low cost.
- Check waiting periods for pre-existing diseases, room-rent limits, co-payment and the network of cashless hospitals.
- Employer cover may end when you change jobs; a personal policy keeps continuity.
- Premiums for self and family qualify for a deduction under the old tax regime.
