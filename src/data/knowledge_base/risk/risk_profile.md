Risk profile combines risk capacity (how much loss your finances can absorb) and risk tolerance (how much decline you can watch without panicking).

- Capacity rises with stable income, long horizons, few dependants and an emergency fund in place.
- Tolerance is emotional: many people who say they are aggressive sell during their first 30% fall.

## Labels often used
- Cautious: protecting capital matters most; accepts lower returns.
- Moderate: accepts some ups and downs for better long-term growth.
- Aggressive: accepts large swings for higher expected returns over long horizons.

A goal's horizon matters too: even an aggressive investor should not keep next year's college fee in small-cap stocks.
