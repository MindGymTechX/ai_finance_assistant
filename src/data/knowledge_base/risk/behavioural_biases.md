---
title: Behavioural biases that hurt investors
category: risk
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: SEBI investor education)
reference_url: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/
---
- Herd mentality: buying because everyone else is, often near market peaks.
- Loss aversion: holding losing stocks too long hoping to "get back to cost", and selling winners too early.
- Overconfidence: trading too often after a few wins.
- Recency bias: assuming recent returns will continue.
- Anchoring: fixing on a past price ("it was ₹500 last year").
- FOMO: chasing hot tips, IPOs or themes on social media.
Simple defences: written goals, an asset allocation, automated SIPs and a yearly review instead of daily checking.
