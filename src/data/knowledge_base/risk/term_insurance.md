---
title: Term life insurance vs investment-linked policies
category: risk
source: FinAI-authored summary (reference: IRDAI consumer education)
reference_url: https://policyholder.gov.in/
---
Term insurance pays a sum to your family only if you die during the policy term. It has no maturity value, which makes it the cheapest way to get large cover.

- How much cover: a common guideline is 10-15 times annual income, plus outstanding loans, minus existing investments.
- Endowment, money-back and many ULIPs combine insurance with investment; they usually provide lower cover and lower returns than buying term insurance and investing separately.
- Insurance is needed mainly if others depend on your income.
